Bell Lap

Your running week, sleep and recovery at a glance.

On Garmin

A watch face for round AMOLED Garmin watches, including Forerunner 165, 265, 570, 965 and 970, Fenix 8, Epix (Gen 2), Venu and Vivoactive. It shows this week's running against your goal, a bar for each day, three data fields you choose and two bezel gauges.

On Apple Watch

An app with complications and Smart Stack widgets for watchOS 10 and later. Put your running week, sleep, VO2 max, heart rate variability and more on the watch face you already use.

Support

To set Bell Lap up:

  • Garmin: on the watch, hold the middle left button, open Watch Face, choose Bell Lap and then Customize. On older watches, change the settings in the Connect IQ app on your phone.
  • Apple Watch: tap the Bell Lap screen to choose what each stat and edge shows. To add a complication, touch and hold your watch face, tap Edit, and pick a Bell Lap reading for a slot.

Something wrong, or a reading you'd like added? Open an issue and describe your watch model and what you see.
